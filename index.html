Ali Abbasi

Vanderbilt University, Department of Computer Science.

Hi, I’m Ali. I’m a computer science Ph.D. candidate at Vanderbilt University, where I work with Prof. Soheil Kolouri in the MINT Lab. Before that I picked up an M.S. at Vanderbilt along the way (2024) and a B.S. in electrical engineering from the University of Tehran (2020). I also spent the summer of 2024 as an intern at Microsoft Research in Redmond, WA.

My research is focused on methods that make large models more efficient to store and run, both at scale in the data center and on resource-constrained edge devices. My recent work covers low-rank factorization of LLMs (ZS-SVD, IO-SVD), activation quantization (ConQuR), and expert selection for mixture-of-experts serving (BASE). My earlier work includes compact reparameterizations of neural networks (PRANC, MCNC) and dataset distillation.

As for hobbies, I enjoy running and lifting weights, though I would cancel all my plans if someone invites me for a poker night.

selected publications

conference papers

  1. NeurIPS
    ConQuR: Corner Aligned Activation Quantization via Optimized Rotations for LLMs
    Thrash, Chayne, Abbasi, Ali, and Kolouri, Soheil
    In Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) 2026
    Abs arXiv

    LLM activation quantization learning optimized rotations that align activations with hypercube corners; 19.2× faster quantization than SpinQuant with lower perplexity across Llama families.

  2. ICML
    Zero Sum SVD: Balancing Loss Sensitivity for Low Rank LLM Compression
    Abbasi, Ali, Thrash, Chayne, Qin, Haoran, Sharma, Shansita, Seifi, Sepehr, and Kolouri, Soheil
    In International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 2026
    Abs arXiv Website

    Selects singular components across all layers with a zero-sum rule on first-order loss estimates; at 40% retention, 5.86× the uncompressed throughput and 16% lower WikiText-2 perplexity than SVD-LLM.

  3. ICLR
    MCNC: Manifold-Constrained Reparameterization for Neural Compression
    Thrash, Chayne, Abbasi, Ali, Andreas, Reed, Nooralinejad, Parsa, Koohpayegani, Soroush Abbasi, Pirsiavash, Hamed, and Kolouri, Soheil
    In International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR) 2025
    Abs arXiv PDF

    Constrains parameters to a low-dimensional nonlinear manifold generated by a frozen random network; +22.3 accuracy points over PLATON pruning at 50× compression (ViT-Ti, ImageNet-100).

  4. CVPR
    BrainWash: A Poisoning Attack to Forget in Continual Learning
    Abbasi, Ali, Nooralinejad, Parsa, Pirsiavash, Hamed, and Kolouri, Soheil
    In IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR) 2024
    Abs arXiv PDF

    Untargeted poisoning attack forcing forgetting in continual learners via model inversion of past data; up to 80% more forgetting than uniform noise.

  5. ICCV
    PRANC: Pseudo Random Networks for Compacting Deep Models
    Nooralinejad, Parsa, Abbasi, Ali, Koohpayegani, Soroush Abbasi, Pourahmadi Meibodi, Kossar, Khan, Rana Muhammad Shahroz, Kolouri, Soheil, and Pirsiavash, Hamed
    In IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV) 2023
    Abs arXiv PDF

    Reparameterizes weights as a linear mixture of frozen random networks regenerated from a seed; +8.1 accuracy points over HashedNet at about 100× compression (ResNet-18, ImageNet-100).

preprints

  1. arXiv
    BASE: Batch-Aware Selection of Experts Using Predicted Removal Error for Efficient MoE Decoding
    Abbasi, Ali, Shi, Justin, and Kolouri, Soheil
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.36222 2026
    Abs arXiv

    Batch-aware MoE expert selection from predicted removal error, with custom GPU kernels; +29.5 accuracy points over the strongest baseline, or 60% faster than dense within 0.4 points (Qwen3-30B-A3B).

  2. arXiv
    IO-SVD: Input-Output Whitened SVD for Adaptive-Rank LLM Compression
    Abbasi, Ali, Thrash, Chayne, Qin, Haoran, Pirsiavash, Hamed, and Kolouri, Soheil
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2605.15626 2026
    Abs arXiv

    LLM/VLM compression via KL-aware input-output whitening and adaptive ranks; +10.2 avg. accuracy points over WSVD (SmolVLM-2B); 4.34× decode speedup with KV-cache compression (LLaMA-2-7B).

workshop papers

    One Category One Prompt: Dataset Distillation using Diffusion Models
    Abbasi, Ali, Shahbazi, Ashkan, Pirsiavash, Hamed, and Kolouri, Soheil

    Abs arXiv

    Distills each class into a learnable text prompt for a diffusion model; +12.6 accuracy points over RDED on ImageNet-1K at 50 images/class (ResNet-18, stored soft labels).

  2. CVPRW

    Abbasi, Ali, Imani, Shima, An, Chenyang, Mahalingam, Gayathri, Shrivastava, Harsh, Diesendruck, Maurice, Pirsiavash, Hamed, Sharma, Pramod, and Kolouri, Soheil
    In CVPR Workshops (SynData4CV) 2026
    Abs arXiv

    Expands compact low-resolution coresets with uncertainty-guided diffusion augmentation; +10.1 accuracy points over RDED on ImageNet-1K at 10 images/class (ResNet-18).
