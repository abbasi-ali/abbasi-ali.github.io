Hi, I'm Ali. I'm a computer science Ph.D. candidate at <a href="https://www.vanderbilt.edu/">Vanderbilt University</a>, where I work with <a href="https://skolouri.github.io/">Prof. Soheil Kolouri</a> in the <a href="https://mint-vu.github.io/">MINT Lab</a>. Before that I picked up an M.S. at Vanderbilt along the way (2024) and a B.S. in electrical engineering from the <a href="https://ut.ac.ir/en">University of Tehran</a> (2020). I also spent the summer of 2024 as an intern at Microsoft Research in Redmond, WA.

My research is focused on methods that make large models more efficient to store and run, both at scale in the data center and on resource-constrained edge devices. My recent work covers low-rank factorization of LLMs (<a href="https://mint-vu.github.io/ZS-SVD/">ZS-SVD</a>, <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15626">IO-SVD</a>), activation quantization (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10793">ConQuR</a>), and expert selection for mixture-of-experts serving (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36222">BASE</a>). My earlier work includes compact reparameterizations of neural networks (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08464">PRANC</a>, <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19301">MCNC</a>) and dataset distillation.

As for hobbies, I enjoy running and lifting weights, though I would cancel all my plans if someone invites me for a poker night.
